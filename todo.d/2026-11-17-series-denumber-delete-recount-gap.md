- [ ] **SERIES-DENUMBER-DELETE-RECOUNT-GAP** `maintenance.series-denumber` deletes an emptied numbered series after counting its book references once more, but the count and the delete are two store calls and no store primitive makes them one atomic step (`DeleteSeries` locks only the name index, and `RenameSeriesIf` is the only compare-and-set). A writer that links a book to the series between the count and the delete leaves that book pointing at a deleted series. The scan stand-down closes the scanner as that writer; other writers are not fenced. Fix options: (1) after the delete, count again and recreate the row under the same id with `RestoreSeries` if a reference appeared (cheap, narrows the window to a repair); (2) add a `DeleteSeriesIfUnreferenced` compare-and-delete to the store interface and both backends (the correct fix; touches the store interface, its generated mocks and every backend). Found in the round-3 review of PR 4698 (item m3) and not done there because it needs a store interface change.
