- [ ] **SERIES-DENUMBER-DELETE-STALE-WRITER-RESIDUE** `maintenance.series-denumber` now deletes an emptied numbered series with the store's `DeleteSeriesIfUnreferenced`, which counts the references and deletes under every book write lock, so no book write can land between the count and the delete. Two windows are left, both outside that call. (1) A book write does not check that the series it names still exists: a writer that read series N before the delete and writes `series_id = N` afterwards strands its own book on a deleted row. Closing it means every writer of `series_id` (the two row writers `createBook` and `updateBookLockedMode`) verifying the series under `nameIdx.series`, which changes the write path of every book edit and the callers that rely on it. (2) The op reads the series row (name, author, hold), journals its undo snapshot, then deletes: a rename or re-author between that read and the delete leaves a snapshot a revert would restore under the old name. Closing it means passing the expected name and author into `DeleteSeriesIfUnreferenced`. Neither is reachable from the op alone while the scan stand-down is held and nobody edits series by hand; both were left out of the round-3 fix of PR 4698 (item m3) because they are different windows from the one that review item named.
