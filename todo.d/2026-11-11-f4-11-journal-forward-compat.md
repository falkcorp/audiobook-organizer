- [ ] **F4-11-JOURNAL-FORWARD-COMPAT** Decide how the revocation journal's line kinds stay forward-compatible. `journal.IsRevocation` counts every kind a build does not know as a revocation (so a newer build's revocation is never skipped), which means a future bookkeeping kind that replays as nothing is also treated as an unapplied revocation by an older build: a rollback onto it stops the boot re-apply at that line, holds the head below it, refuses live appends and fails closed once. Either add a format version to the journal header (an older build refuses a newer version outright with one clear error) or a declared-skippable rule (a kind prefix or header list that marks a line as bookkeeping an older build may pass); amend 05-backup section 5B.3.6 and 5B.9 with whichever it is. The known revocations after an unknown line are already applied (F4-11 decision 28), so this is about not forcing the fail-closed and the refusal on a rollback, not about losing revocations.
