- [ ] **04-P7 opchange ensure timeout** Same question as the 04-P6 fresh-install boot ops note, for the fifth boot-enqueued op: `maintenance.opchange-book-index-rebuild` with `{"ensure": true}` (60 min timeout) walks the whole operation-change journal on every boot to verify the `opchange_by_book:` index, and a first build or a rebuild after a rollback walks it again. The journal is never pruned by log retention (see UNDO-WINDOW-PRUNE), so the walk only grows. A run cut by the timeout fails red and leaves the index untrusted (reads use the full scan); the next boot resumes the backfill from its cursor, but a rebuild restarts from the first row. Decide whether the ensure needs a longer timeout, or whether journal pruning (UNDO-WINDOW-PRUNE) is the fix.
