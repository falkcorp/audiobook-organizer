- [ ] **04-P7 opchange ensure timeout** Same question as the 04-P6 fresh-install boot ops note, for the fifth boot-enqueued op: `maintenance.opchange-book-index-rebuild` with `{"ensure": true}` (60 min timeout). Its backfill and rebuild are resumable: each commits its cursor with every chunk, and a cut rebuild leaves the sentinel unset, so the next boot's ensure backfill resumes from that cursor (`backfillOpChangeByBook` in `internal/database/pebble_store_opchange_index.go`). The verify is not resumable: it re-walks the whole operation-change journal on every boot, and must finish inside one run before the index is trusted. The journal is never pruned by log retention (see UNDO-WINDOW-PRUNE), so the verify only grows. If it ever takes longer than the timeout, every boot fails red and the index is never trusted (reads stay on the full scan, correct but slow). Decide whether the ensure needs a longer timeout, a resumable verify (checkpoint the verify cursor), or whether journal pruning (UNDO-WINDOW-PRUNE) is the fix.
