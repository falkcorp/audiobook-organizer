- [ ] **LOG-RETENTION-DAYS-NO-UPPER-BOUND** `log_retention_days` has no upper bound, and `logger.PruneOldLogs` (`internal/logger/retention.go`) computes `time.Now().AddDate(0, 0, -retentionDays)` from it. Measured with Go's time package: at 1<<60 days the cutoff comes out equal to now and at math.MaxInt it comes out the day after now, so the purge deletes every `operationlog:` and `syslog:` row (fail-open). Only an absurd configured value reaches it, but nothing in `config.Validate` refuses one. Fix: cap `log_retention_days` in `Validate` (the core journal's 36500 is the precedent, `logjournal.MaxRetentionDays`), and have `PruneOldLogs` refuse, deleting nothing, a cutoff that is not strictly before now. Found by the F5-03 security review of the core prune kind, which does not have it.
