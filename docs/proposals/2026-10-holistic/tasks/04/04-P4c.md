## Goal
Merge `scheduler.metadata-refresh` into `maintenance.metadata-refresh`. The `scheduler.*` def and its body are deleted, the task retargets to the survivor, the loser ID becomes a `FormerIDs` alias on the survivor, and the survivor now declares `Permissions: settings.manage` and the larger of the two timeouts, so the merge neither widens who can run a deleting op nor shortens a budget prod runs were sized against.

If it holds, keep `ResumeRequeue` and write the justification in the PR body; if the body writes, set the survivor to `ResumeDrop`.
