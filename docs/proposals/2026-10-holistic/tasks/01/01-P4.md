## Goal
The `opstate:<id>:params` Pebble side table has two writers and zero readers. After this PR nothing writes it: the organizer stops saving an empty struct on every organize, and the iTunes importer stops saving its import parameters. The store methods `SaveOperationParams` and `GetOperationParams` and the helpers that wrap them are deleted. The `opstate:` STATE half (checkpoints) is live and stays.
