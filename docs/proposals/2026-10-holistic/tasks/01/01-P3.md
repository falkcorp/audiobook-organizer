## Goal
The minute-by-minute reaper that unmarshals and sorts the whole v1 `operation:` keyspace (1,440 full scans a day, and it can never match anything because v1 has had no writer since 2026-08-23) is gone. `GET /operations/pause` reports running pausable and non-pausable ops from the v2 table, so the two fields are no longer always empty. The unrouted `Handler.CancelOperation` and `Handler.resolveScheduler` are deleted.

7. Fragment: the pause response now lists running ops (it was always empty); the unused minute-by-minute scan is gone.
