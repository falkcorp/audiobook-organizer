The **Authors & series** lane gains an **AI scans** sub-view: start a realtime or batch AI author scan, follow progress in the notification bell, cancel, browse scan history (including nightly scans marked superseded), filter results by agreement with clickable counts, select results and apply. ## Steps
1. Baseline: run `DedupAIReviewTab.test.tsx` and note the two case names to port.
2. Segmented control becomes **Authors | Series | AI scans**. `?view=ai` seeds the third, once, never mirrored.
3. Sub-view body: a scan picker as a visible **history list** (not a drawer) so superseded nightly scans are findable without hunting; each entry shows its date, mode (realtime/batch) and a **superseded** marker where the server says so. Selecting a scan loads its results.
4. Agreement filter chips **All / Agreed / Groups Only / Full Only / Disagreed**, each showing a count and clickable (rule: every count opens its rows). Switching scan or filter clears the selection (copy `DedupAIReviewTab.tsx:58-69`).
5. Selection and **Apply selected (N)**: only results that are applicable are selectable; applying a superseded scan is refused with the server's message shown (ported case).
6. Lane-menu commands: **Start AI author scan** (Realtime or Batch per the switch; keep the two switch labels `Realtime (faster, more expensive)` and `Batch (cheaper, hours)`), and **Cancel scan**. Both use `startJob` so progress appears in the notification bell; do not copy the page-local poll loop (`:100-120`). Do not port the "Books" placeholder tab.
7. Port tests and the E2E block (failing first).
