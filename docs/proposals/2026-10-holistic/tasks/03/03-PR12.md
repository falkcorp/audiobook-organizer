## Goal
The five routes (plus two deprecated aliases) that only the old Version Groups and Duplicate Scan tabs called stop doing work: they answer `410 Gone` with a short JSON body for one release, their handlers and the now-unused service/store method are removed, and the stale `scripts/api_examples.sh` example is repointed. The PR contains a recorded proof that no caller anywhere (browser, script, tool, runbook, AudioBooth) still uses them.

**Parity-matrix rows closed** (ids from section 3.1 of the spec): C10 (backend side; the UI side was closed as obsolete in PR 11), C04 and C06 remain covered by the Duplicates lane.
