- Metadata search: the "stop at the first rung that answers" ladder is replaced by a multi-combination fan-out that runs on every search, interactive and batch. One book's title is cleaned (`parseSearchTitle`: the organizer's " - Unknown Author" suffix, "read by X (Title)", a trailing "(Narrator)", "Series 17: Title", "Series - 4 - Title", "Series, Book 5 - Author", a leading year, "(Unabridged)", a genre subtitle) and turned into up to 4 provider questions, ordered by expected value: title + author, title + narrator (taggers swap the two), the existing anchored ladder variants, the title without a leading year or subtitle, series + author kept only at the parsed position, and title alone. Audible and Open Library are asked every variant. Google Books (a 1,000/day key quota), Hardcover and Wikipedia are asked only the best one. Audnexus has no title search, so it is no longer title-searched: before, every rung spent a request on its `/authors` endpoint and got nothing back. It is asked only by ASIN. Variants run in rounds, and the fan-out stops after the first round that pools a strong match: the book's own ASIN, or the title, a person of the book's and a runtime within 15%.
- Search ranking: results are pooled across variants and sources and deduplicated by ASIN/ISBN. A result with no ID is deduplicated by normalized title + author, so two editions of one title both reach the ranking. A result carrying the book's own ASIN is ranked first. A result whose author is the book's narrator gets a swapped credit (x1.4) instead of the 0.7 author-mismatch penalty. The series and position read from the title count toward series scoring. The book's own ASIN is now looked up whenever it has one, not only when the book has no usable author. The lookup is skipped when the pool already holds that ASIN with a runtime.
- The fetch cache now keys every variant separately (`<provider>#q<digest>` rows under the book's existing prefix). It still honors `SearchOptions.BypassFetchCache` and still never caches an empty answer. The pooled per-provider row is still written for the single-book and bulk fetch paths. `searchInputVersion` is bumped to "2", so every cached "nothing" verdict is asked again once.
- Candidate fetch op sizing: `metafetch.SourcesBudget` now reports `CallsPerBook`, `BindingID` and `BooksPerSec` from the search's own per-source call policy (`MaxSearchCallsPerBook`), and the op's log names the binding source. An ASIN-only source (Audnexus) no longer sets the worker queue cap.
