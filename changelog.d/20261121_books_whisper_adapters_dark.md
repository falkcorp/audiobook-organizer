### Added

- `internal/books/adapters/whisper`, books' two transcription adapters for core's `ai` integration kind, as a dark slice of the F7-09 integrations flip (T4): nothing registers them yet, so nothing changes at runtime until the fold. `books.whisper_server` speaks the existing whisper worker protocol natively over the instance's outbound client: it reads `/health` to measure the worker's model, its device as `cuda`/`metal`/`gpu`/`cpu` labels and a `batch` label, and a `shared_fs:<root>` feature only for library roots the instance maps and the worker reports readable, uploads a clip to `/transcribe` or sends a root-relative library path to `/transcribe-path-batch`, and classifies every failure (status codes, the worker's per-clip `<code>: <detail>` errors, a worker too old to serve the path route) so the router, not the adapter, decides failover and benching. `books.whisper_local` runs a clip through a caller-supplied `Runner` (books wires one over its uv launcher), one clip at a time, and always reports itself local. Neither adapter retries.
