- Metadata search, placeholder authors: "Unknown Author", "read by narrator" and the other system placeholders are no longer sent to providers as the author a search is narrowed by, whether they came from the batch fetch's hint or from the search ladder refilling the author from the book's AuthorID. A book with no usable author is searched by title plus narrator, and its own ASIN is looked up directly when it has one. The bulk metadata fetch strips them too. The apply gate's identity check accepts a cache row hashed with no author only when the book's author is a placeholder, so rows written this way do not read as identity drift, and the candidate-fetch "no match" line names the author actually searched instead of the stored placeholder.
- Metadata search, placeholder and chapter titles: books titled "", "Unknown Title", "Chapter 3", "03" or "06 Chapter 6" are no longer skipped by the batch candidate fetch or left out of the bulk metadata fetch. Both use one predicate (`metadata.IsUnsearchableTitle`) and one resolver (`metabatch.ResolveCandidateSearchQuery`) that searches by the book's transcribed title, then a file's transcribed title, then the work folder's name (`metadata.ChapterTitleFromDirectory`). A book is skipped only when none is usable, with a per-book reason line and, on the bulk path, a `skipped_no_title` or `skipped_fragment` ledger row. The stand-in title is only a query: it is never written onto the book. The bulk path resolves it inside its worker pool and keys the provider fetch cache on it, as the per-book search already did.
- Bulk apply gate, transcription-found candidates: a candidate found by searching the book's transcribed title, whose title matches that transcription (normalized equality and the shared title matcher), now passes the certainty gate's title check and, when the caller proves the cache row differs from the book only in that query, its identity leg. It is still refused on an ASIN conflict, a partial book, and identity_stale for any other reason (an author change, a row fetched for another query, a book that has since been given a real title). The transcription counts as one agreement, not two, so it cannot pass the evidence leg on its own. The evidence is recorded on the candidate and the gate verdict (`identity_evidence`) and in the bulk apply's applied and refused lines. A candidate found by the folder-name fallback gets no such evidence and stays identity_stale in bulk apply.
