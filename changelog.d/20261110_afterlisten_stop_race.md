### Fixed

- Server stop no longer races the post-listen start. The kernel runs the listener's `Serve` on its own goroutine and can begin the stop before it ran, or end the listener drain while `afterListen` is still starting the scheduler, ticker, watchers and session cleanup, so `StopHook`'s wait on the background WaitGroup overlapped those adds and a caller that changed config after the stop raced `afterListen`'s read of it (the flaky `TestRestoreOfflineArchive_R1` in the `-race` shard). A gate now seals the post-listen start at the first stop step: a start that has not begun never runs, and one that has is told to stop at its next step and is waited for, within its own share of the shutdown budget. A start that outlives that share is reported as an abandoned step, and the watcher stop, the background-goroutine waits and the scheduler wait run behind the gate instead of racing it. The file-op recovery and its backpressure wait now end at a stop, and afterListen reads the auto-scan settings StartHook captured instead of the process-global config. `Start`'s final stop now has a 30 s deadline.
