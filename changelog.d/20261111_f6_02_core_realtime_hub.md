### Added

- core/realtime: the core realtime hub and its one stream, `GET /api/core/v1/events` (dark: nothing mounts it until F6-03). `core/realtime` builds a hub over the topic catalog's Realtime topics, registers one raw subscription per topic with the bus (coalescing topics through the bus's own latest-wins window) and serves server-sent events with resume by `Last-Event-ID`: a numbered ring of the last 1024 events per process, an id of `<bootNonce>-<seq>`, and a `core.stream.reset.v1` frame (restart, gap or overflow) when a client cannot be resumed. Every event is judged for the caller at send time, live or replayed alike: the topic's permission against the caller's checker as it stands now, then the topic's `Visible` filter, then its `Redact`, each handed its own copy of the payload and contained if it panics. A Confined key or a principal with no compiled checker is refused (403) and never streams; unknown topics are 400, an exact topic the caller may not see is 403, and the stream limits answer 429 (per user and credential class) or 503 with `Retry-After`, all before the first stream byte. Streams revalidate the credential and re-resolve the principal (a required `Deps.Refresh`, so a role change reaches an open stream within one heartbeat) on every heartbeat and end on `core.principal.invalidated.v1` or a rights change; while a refresh is failing a stream delivers nothing and is sent a reset when it recovers; a shutdown or revocation interrupts a write in flight; shutdown sends `core.stream.closed.v1` to every stream and drains inside its budget. Every bound is fixed at construction: subscribers globally and per user and class, the per-subscriber queue, the ring, `Last-Event-ID` and query lengths, and the payload size; a slow reader is reset, never waited for, and a write past its deadline is disconnected. Eight metric families under the core `realtime` scope.
- core/internal/sse: the engine under the hub (ring, per-subscriber bounded queue, wake channel, heartbeat, per-write deadline, frame writer), with no knowledge of topics or permissions. core/internal/eventhttp mounts the route on the core group as a raw `AnyAuthenticated` route.
- core/tasks: `RunViewRedactor`, the catalog `Redact` for `core.task.run-changed.v1`, which applies the same `RedactRunView` the REST run routes use.
