### Added

- core: the authorization service (`core/rbac`, dark): the SDK role `Store` and `Importer`, the native store `core/internal/rbacstore` over `c/rbac/`, the `rbactest.StoreSuite` conformance kit, catalog assembly and `Freeze`, role CRUD with the escalation, last-admin and role-in-use rules, `CanAssign`, `Dominates`, the checker cache in front of the injected `Deps.Compile`, `Can`/`Require`/`RequireAll` with denial audit, the seed merge of the core seed roles with restricted additions held for acceptance (`AcceptSeed`), and the confinement and ambient rules (F4-01; nothing calls it until F4-08). Every authorization error path fails closed: one matching predicate (`rbac.Covers`) decides what a pattern grants, so `core:admin.*` counts as an admin grant everywhere; the ambient rule refuses a set it cannot judge; a seed role confined through a wildcard is refused, and a stored worker role cannot leave confinement through an emptied or undeclared seed without an administrator's acceptance; `Freeze` seals the role importer; and `Dominates` refuses a target role it does not know.
