Five per-book audiobook routes are retired: the alternative-titles routes (`GET`, `POST` and `DELETE /api/v1/audiobooks/:id/alternative-titles`), `GET /api/v1/audiobooks/:id/path-history` and the old `POST /api/v1/audiobooks/:id/rescan` alias. Nothing in the web app, scripts or the AudioBooth client called them. They now answer 410 Gone for one release; path history is already part of the book changelog, and `/rescan` was only an alias for `reconcile-files`, which keeps working. Their handlers and tests are deleted. The stored alternative titles and path history are untouched and still used by search, dedup and the changelog.
