### Changed

- The tests in `internal/server/handlers` and its `abs`, `aibackends`, `audiobooks`, `dedup`, `metadata` and `system` sub-packages no longer assign to or read the package-global `config.AppConfig` directly. They set configuration through `config.SetForTest` and `config.ReplaceForTest` and read it through `config.Get()`. Test-only change; no behaviour change in the application.
