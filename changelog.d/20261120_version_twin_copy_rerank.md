### Fixed

- The version-twin metadata repair (`maintenance.version-twin-metadata`) now re-ranks the candidate row it copies onto a primary book by that book's own owner rejections. Before, a candidate the owner had rejected for the primary could sit first in the copied row until the next search or reject rewrote it (every apply still refused it). The copy, the re-rank and the journal row happen in that order; the journaled stamp does not depend on candidate order, so the op revert is unchanged. A failed rejection read, or a book that vanished, is reported as `repairs.ErrPartiallyApplied` after the written copy has been journaled, never ranked as if nothing were rejected. `VersionTwinMetadataService` gains `RerankCachedCandidates`, which `*metafetch.Service` already had.
