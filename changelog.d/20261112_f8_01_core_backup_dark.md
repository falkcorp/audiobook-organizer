### Added

- core: `core/backup` (dark), the platform-core backup and restore of every core store. It writes the `mediacore-backup/1` archive (a manifest first, one SHA-256 per entry, a keyring-derived MAC bound to the install), keeps the catalogue and retention (the newest verified archive and a pending restore's archive are never deleted; retention runs before the space check), verifies at three levels (checksum, entries, open), and stages a restore in the one swap slot after provenance, compatibility, upgrade-floor and secrets-coverage checks. It reads HEAD's `legacy-abo/0` archives for the staged and the offline restore, as foreign restores, and registers the `core.backup.*` tasks, the `backup` metrics scope and (in `core/internal/backuphttp`, unmounted) the `/api/core/v1/backups` routes. Archives never hold a plaintext secret or the keyring, every tar entry is bounded and must be a regular file, and a privileged restore option (foreign, replace-config, unreadable-secrets, weak-verify) needs an interactive-owner proof that only the route supplies, so the task path cannot pass one. ABO's own backup and routes are unchanged.
- core: `storage restore-offline --archive` takes `--keyring <file>` (the keyring export, mode 0600) and `--gap-fill <file>` (the off-host `config.yaml`), staged under the state-file labels `keyring` and `gap_fill`; the files they replace are set aside, never deleted, and an archive without them leaves the live files alone. A legacy archive restored without `--gap-fill` warns what then migrates empty. An app that sets no `R1ArchiveExtract` now extracts archives through core's extractor, which writes only the store (`db/`) of a `mediacore-backup/1` archive.
