### Added

- Books: the config and secrets cutover M2, shipped dark and unregistered (platform-core F3-06), in `internal/books/cutover`. A generated rename table maps every HEAD setting to its core key, secret, entry list or retired name (330 rows, with a golden file), and frozen copies of HEAD's settings types, blob decoder, effective-value rules and AES-GCM cipher let M2a read the four encrypted rows and the blob. One chunk moves the credentials into core's secrets store under their canonical names, stages the keys (the two `FailClosed` ones at their defaults too), quarantines what has no place (sealed, with a fixed reason that never carries a value), deletes the rows and rewrites the blob down to what M6 still needs. A missing or short `.encryption_key` with an encrypted row refuses the upgrade with exit 78 before anything is written, unless `ABO_CORE_SECRETS_ALLOW_KEY_REGEN` is set. M2b (`StripGapFill`) removes the fifteen managed keys from `config.yaml` after M2a commits. The books level step and M8 are built here too, with `ValidateCutoverList`. Nothing runs on `main` until F3-07 registers it.
