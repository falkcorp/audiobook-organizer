### Added

- Books: the config and secrets cutover M2, shipped dark and unregistered (platform-core F3-06), in `internal/books/cutover`. A generated rename table maps every HEAD setting to its core key, secret, entry list or retired name (330 rows, with a golden file), and frozen copies of HEAD's settings types, blob decoder, effective-value rules and AES-GCM cipher let M2a read the four encrypted rows and the blob. One chunk moves the credentials into core's secrets store under their canonical names, stages the keys (the two `FailClosed` ones at their defaults too), quarantines what has no place (sealed, with a fixed reason that never carries a value), deletes the rows and rewrites the blob down to what M6 still needs. A credential embedded in a URL (`user:pass@host`, in a key or in a list element such as a Whisper endpoint) moves to a secret next to the key; a URL that cannot be proven free of one is quarantined sealed and never staged. The upgrade is refused before anything is written when an encrypted row has no key (searched in the state directory, then the database's directory), when `config.yaml` exists but cannot be read, or when no settings blob exists and the file or per-key rows hold settings nothing would import, each with the way out. M2b (`StripGapFill`) removes the fifteen managed keys from `config.yaml` after M2a commits, writing the file as HEAD's saver does (symlinks followed, mode and owner kept) and keeping any gap-fill value whose destination does not hold it yet. The books level step and M8 are built here too, with `ValidateCutoverList`. Nothing runs on `main` until F3-07 registers it.
