- Repairs lane: a "Junk titles" fixer replaces the `maintenance.repair-junk-titles` op, which matched only five exact strings and was never applied. A new classifier (`metadata.ClassifyJunkTitle`) finds chapter positions ("01", "Chapter 12", "Disc 2", "Part IV", "Chapter One"), narrator credits ("read by …"), track tags ("Opening", "Intro", "Big Finish Ident"), placeholders ("Unknown Title", "Untitled"), bare roman numerals, and titles behind a track-number or punctuation prefix ("01 - Eldest"). Real titles such as "1984", "11/22/63", "Book 3", "I, Robot", "l'Étranger", "V for Vendetta" and "'Salem's Lot" are left alone. Each row proposes a title and names its source. Sources are tried in this order: the intro transcription, a cached metadata candidate with the same author, a score of at least 0.5 and no runtime mismatch, the title without its prefix, the folder or filename, then the series and number. Some books are chapter files of a bigger book. These are listed as "fragment — use the consolidation fixer" and are not retitled: a chapter-titled single file, a book sharing its folder with another, a book whose file belongs to another book, a book whose author is its folder's name, or a proposal that duplicates an existing or co-proposed title of the same author. Other skipped rows are listed as "needs manual", "user-locked", iTunes, or Doctor Who / Big Finish / Torchwood. Apply writes the title only, records history so "undo last apply" restores it, refuses a title that changed since the trial, and passes over rows already written when an interrupted apply is re-run.
- Repairs lane: a "Letter-l ordinals" fixer rewrites numbered-book titles typed with the letter l ("l Corinthians", "ll Kings") to digits ("1 Corinthians", "2 Kings").
- Repairs lane: the Skipped tab now shows one chip per skip kind with its count, and each chip opens exactly those rows. The rows endpoint accepts `filter=skipped:<kind>`.
- Title sort: "I Corinthians", "1 Corinthians", "First Corinthians" and "l Corinthians" now sort together as "Corinthians 1", and the same holds for the other numbered books (Samuel, Kings, Chronicles, Esdras, Maccabees, Thessalonians, Timothy, Peter, John). This applies to the library's title sort (memdb index and materialised sort), ABS `titleIgnorePrefix` and the Works table. The rewrite only fires in front of a known numbered-book name, so "V for Vendetta", "I, Robot" and "l'Étranger" sort as before.
