### Fixed

- The Account merges panel can undo a merge made under the new account system and resume one whose last sweep did not finish. A refused undo now says why in words (the merged-away account changed, was deleted or re-merged, the account that stayed is disabled, an address, key or sign-in moved, no active admin would remain, or the merge must be resumed first). A guest's Cloudflare access and phone key are not restored by an undo and must be reissued.
