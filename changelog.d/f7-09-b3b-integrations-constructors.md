Added, dark, the kernel constructors the F7 integrations flip will call (`core/kernel/integrations_*.go`): the build of providerhttp, the integrations Registry, the AI router, the library manager, the download pool and the notify service in a fixed order with a reverse close, the adapter registration and load, the health task body, the integration and library route mounts, the five storage owners and the two topics the flip adds. Nothing calls them yet, so no behaviour changes in the shipped binary; each is covered by tests against a real store, and the wiring that calls them is the next change. Two small changes reach beyond the dark constructors: `integrations.Options.HealthChanged` now takes a `first` flag and is called while the Registry holds its health lock (so two concurrent probes cannot publish out of order), and `core.download.caps_ttl` is now a restart-class key, because the download pool reads it once when it is built.
