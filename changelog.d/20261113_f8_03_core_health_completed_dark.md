### Added

- core: `core/health` is completed, dark (05-health §5H.3 to §5H.10). The registry now runs each component's probe on its own interval with a bound, so a panicking or hanging probe becomes Down (`check_panic`, `check_timeout`) instead of stalling the loop; a heartbeat goroutine feeds `/livez` and the systemd watchdog, and a stalled one fails `/livez` and stops the pings. Readiness is `/readyz` (503 until startup completes, 503 as soon as shutdown begins and before the HTTP drain, 503 while a Critical component is Down) and the unauthenticated bodies carry only fixed tokens. `health.Startup` runs a table of named steps, refuses a step it has no row for, and sends `EXTEND_TIMEOUT_USEC` once per progressing step, never on a timer. The stop budget splits the 25 s total across phases so the phases can never add up to more than the total, with the 8 s container default, and a bounded phase that ignores its deadline is abandoned at it so one stuck drain cannot carry the shutdown past the total. The readiness status is declared as the bus topic `core.system.status-changed.v1` (bus only, one-second realtime coalescing, `core:system.status`, the `/readyz` body as its payload). Eight metric families (component status, check duration and failures, ready, startup degraded, diagnostics bundles and redactions, shutdown phase seconds) have a golden file.
- core: `core/health/diagnostics` writes the support bundle: a zip of per-section JSONL files, sections filtered by what the requester may see, three redaction layers (typed secret placeholders, a pattern scan that also covers map keys and absolute paths, permission), bounded lines and files, every path through an `os.Root`, and a download that re-filters against the downloader, is refused to anyone but the run's actor or `core:admin`, needs an interactive session, and expires with the retention window. `core/health/sysinfo` collects host and process facts for `/api/core/v1/system/info`. `core/internal/healthhttp` builds the routes and the `/livez` and `/readyz` registration, unmounted.
- core: `archtest.StartupSteps` fails a build when a `Startup.Run` step name is not a row of the table, is not a literal, or a row is never run.
- Nothing is switched: `/health`, `/api/health`, `/api/v1/health` and `/api/v1/system/status` answer as before, and the kernel boot path is unchanged. Wiring is F8-04.
