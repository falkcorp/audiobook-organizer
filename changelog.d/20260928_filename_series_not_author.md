- Filename and folder parsing no longer file a series or title as the author. "The Stormlight Archive - The Way of Kings" in a folder with no author now yields series "The Stormlight Archive" and title "The Way of Kings" instead of author "The Stormlight Archive". A side that opens with "The"/"A"/"An" (initials like "A J Finn" excepted) or contains a measured series word (Archive, Chronicles, Series, Cycle, Trilogy, Collection, Omnibus, Book, Vol, Chapter, Part, Disc) is never taken as the author, in the " - " and "_" filename paths, the folder-name fallback, or the folder parser. "Saga" is not on the list: it is a surname (Junichi Saga).
- A side refused only because it opens with an article can still be a real credit ("An Na", "The Arbinger Institute"), so it is now filed as neither the series nor the title: "An Na - A Step from Heaven" gives title "A Step from Heaven" with no author and no series, where it briefly gave series "An Na". A zero-padded volume number marks "Series NN - Title" ("The Expanse 01 - Leviathan Wakes" no longer credits "Leviathan Wakes"), unless the right side names one of the file's own folders, as it does in the organizer's "<author>/<title>/<title> - <author>" layout. The folder parser stops its author walk at a work-named folder, so "/mnt/Science Fiction/The Stormlight Archive/..." no longer credits "Science Fiction".
- Limits that remain: "The Hunger Games - Catching Fire" still gives author "Catching Fire" (the same shape as "The Stand - Stephen King"). "Discworld 01 - Terry Pratchett" outside a "Terry Pratchett" folder gives title "Terry Pratchett" and no author. In an "<author>/<series>/<title>/<disc>" folder layout the author folder is no longer reached when the series folder is work-named ("Stephen King/The Dark Tower/The Gunslinger/Disc 1" gives no folder author; before this change it gave "The Dark Tower"). "An Na", "A Johnston" and "The Hobbit - Tolkien" get no author. In every case the author is left empty for tags or AI nomination to fill, rather than set wrong.
