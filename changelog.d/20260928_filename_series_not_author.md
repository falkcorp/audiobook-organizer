- Filename parsing no longer reads a series or title as the author. "The Stormlight Archive - The Way of Kings" in a folder with no author now yields series "The Stormlight Archive" and title "The Way of Kings" instead of author "The Stormlight Archive". A side that opens with "The"/"A"/"An" (initials like "A J Finn" excepted) or contains a series word (Archive, Chronicles, Saga, Series, Cycle, Trilogy, Collection, Book, Vol, Chapter...) can no longer be the author, in the " - " and "_" filename paths, the folder-name fallback, or the folder parser (`looksLikeAuthorSegment`, which took a series folder in a series/title/disc layout as the author). `personname.LooksLikeWorkTitle` is new; the leading-article tiebreak in `ChooseAuthorSide` is gone because the new check does its job earlier. Known cost: an author whose name starts with the word "An" ("An Na") gets no author, and is left for AI nomination.
