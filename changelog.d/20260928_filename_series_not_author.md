- Filename and folder parsing no longer file a series or title as the author. "The Stormlight Archive - The Way of Kings" in a folder with no author now yields series "The Stormlight Archive" and title "The Way of Kings" instead of author "The Stormlight Archive". A side that opens with "The"/"A"/"An" (initials like "A J Finn" excepted) or contains a measured series word (Archive, Chronicles, Series, Cycle, Trilogy, Collection, Omnibus, Book, Vol, Chapter, Part, Disc) or a zero-padded number is never taken as the author, in the " - " and "_" filename paths, the folder-name fallback, or the folder parser. "Saga" is not on the list: it is a surname (Junichi Saga).
- A side refused only because it opens with an article can still be a real credit ("An Na", "The Arbinger Institute"), so it is filed as neither the series nor the title: "An Na - A Step from Heaven" gives title "A Step from Heaven" with no author and no series. A position marker is not a title: "The Hobbit - Chapter 01" / "- Part 1" / "- 01" give title "The Hobbit". "Mistborn Book 1 - The Final Empire" gives series "Mistborn Book 1" (stored as "Mistborn", position 1) and title "The Final Empire"; titles carrying a series word ("The Book Thief") are not series.
- A zero-padded volume number on the left marks "Series NN - Title" ("The Expanse 01 - Leviathan Wakes" no longer credits "Leviathan Wakes"), except in the organizer's own "<author>/<title>/<title> - <author>" layout (immediate folder = left side, a folder above = right side), and except when the right side is a strong name (initials, "Surname, Given", a list of names, or three or more words).
- When the scanner already has an author from a tag, the filename split is oriented by it: the side matching the tag (ignoring case, an edition suffix, initial punctuation and "Surname, Given" order) is the author and the other side the title; a parsed author that disagrees with the tag is refused and the pair reads as series - title ("The Hunger Games - Catching Fire" with tag Suzanne Collins gives series "The Hunger Games", title "Catching Fire").
- The folder parser refuses genre and container folders (Science Fiction, Fantasy, Mystery, Thriller, Horror, Romance, Fiction, Non-Fiction, Biography, History, Young Adult, Kids, Children, Audiobooks, Books, Media, Library, Downloads, ...) as authors everywhere in its walk. When it meets a work-named folder it records it as the series and looks exactly one folder further out for the author, then stops: "Stephen King/The Dark Tower/The Gunslinger/Disc 1" gives author "Stephen King" and series "The Dark Tower"; "/mnt/Science Fiction/The Stormlight Archive/..." gives series "The Stormlight Archive" and no author.
- The directory fallback no longer reads a title folder as the author when the file's folder is named exactly the book's title ("Neil Gaiman/Good Omens/Good Omens.mp3" gives "Neil Gaiman", not "Good Omens"). A title folder whose name differs from the parsed title is still read as the author; todo.d/20260825-directory-fallback-reads-title-as-author.md stays open for that.
- Limits that remain: without a tag, "The Hunger Games - Catching Fire" gives author "Catching Fire" and "Andy Weir - Project Hail Mary" gives author "Project Hail Mary" (both sides are person-shaped; the "Title - Author" convention decides, as before this change). Without a tag, a plain two-word name after a padded number outside the organizer layout ("The Dark Tower 01 - Stephen King", "Discworld 01 - Terry Pratchett") gives that name as the title and no author. A person-shaped genre folder not on the stop-list (e.g. "Space Opera") can still be taken as an author. "An Na", "A Johnston" and "The Hobbit - Tolkien" get no author. Where the author is refused it is left empty for tags or AI nomination to fill.
