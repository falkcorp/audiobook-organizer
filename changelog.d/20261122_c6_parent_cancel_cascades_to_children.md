### Fixed

- Cancelling a run now cancels the runs it enqueued, as HEAD's cancel stopped the work a job had started (F5-18 C6). Before this a cancelled batch parent stopped after its current child, and that child kept writing until its own timeout. The executor now ends every unfinished child of the cancelled run (and theirs): the child's context is cancelled with a cause that names its parent, its writer refuses from the next write, and its row ends canceled. A child queued while the cancel was being applied is cancelled at dispatch instead of started, and after a restart in the middle of a cancel no child is resumed: each is ended canceled at boot. Only a cancel cascades (not the watchdog, a timeout or a shutdown), and only down to the run's own children that the person cancelling could cancel directly: a child whose kind needs a permission they lack is left running, and the cancel names it in the parent's run log and in the `core.task.cancel` audit record (`skipped`). Known gap: a queued skipped child is still ended at its next dispatch, and one unfinished at a restart is ended at boot, because those two paths hold no permission check yet. This is dark until the tasks flip: the executor is not started in the app today.
