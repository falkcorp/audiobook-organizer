- The Authors & series lane in Review has a Series sub-view (`/review?lane=authors&view=series`, or the Authors | Series control). It lists series that share a name, with inline rename (undoable: the toast and the row both offer Undo), a per-author narrator flag, Validate, and Merge, Merge Selected and Merge All. Merge All takes the same-name groups only and lists every group in its confirmation; sub-series groups are merged one at a time. Each merge keeps the first series that is still included (author-linked first), and a series can be left out of it. The list is edited in place after a write, with a stale banner when the server's copy is gone, and a merge that fails part-way keeps only the series that are still there. Prune Series is not part of this lane.
- A series merge or Merge All that is interrupted by an expired login or an unanswered request reports how many groups merged and how many were never sent, and leaving the lane names the merges that were not sent.
- `dedup.series-dedup` no longer reports success when an apply kept a duplicate series (a refused or failed delete); it fails after invalidating the cache and names the series ids it kept. `dedup.series-merge` failures now begin with the ids it left behind, in a fixed form the web lane reads.
