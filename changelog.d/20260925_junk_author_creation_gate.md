### Fixed

- Junk author names are no longer created or linked. A single shared gate, `personname.IsPlausibleAuthorName`, refuses HTML entities, copyright lines, "N short stories", Star Wars era tags ("14 BBY"), names opening with punctuation ("- Epigraph", "[PZG]"), structural labels ("Epigraph", "Book 1", "Vol. 01"), "(Unabridged)", "read by …" / "narrated by …", timecodes and track numbers, bare numbers ("02-25", "2"), placeholders ("Unknown", "Various", "n/a") and strings over 80 characters. The scanner (tag, folder and AI parse), metadata batch update, provider apply, refetch-missing-authors, importer, AI cover analysis, per-book metadata fetch and author-path-link all check it before looking a name up, and `CreateAuthor` enforces it as the last line of defence with a typed `ErrImplausibleAuthorName`. The canonical "Unknown Author" fallback is exempt. Over the 15,055 production author names the gate refuses 658, with no real names among them.
- The author split ops no longer drop a real co-author's credit when one part fails to create; a part refused by the gate is dropped on purpose, any other failure leaves the composite alone.
- `SplitCompositeAuthorName` no longer splits space-joined word runs ("Wraith Knight Three Worlds" became two authors). Of 349 production names that heuristic split, about 8 were real two-person credits, and it split "Joseph Sheridan Le Fanu". Every part a split returns now also passes the creation gate.
