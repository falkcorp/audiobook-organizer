### Added

- Tests that pin how a Cloudflare Access sign-in finds its account when two administrators share one address and only one of them is linked to Access. A sign-in whose subject is the Access token's opaque `sub` reaches the linked account directly; the same person under an e-mail-shaped subject, or a subject never seen before, is refused with nothing created, because the address alone cannot choose between two administrators. The verifier's e-mail fallback for the subject is shown to apply only to a token with no `sub`. The address rules for an unlinked sign-in are each pinned at full boot: two proven holders, an administrator holder and a holder with a password are refused, a single plain proven holder is linked, and two unproven holders give a new viewer without displacing the unproven administrator. After the upgrade both same-address administrators keep their own ids, roles and proven flag, the Access link stays on the linked one only, and no sign-in path merges accounts.
