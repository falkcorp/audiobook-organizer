In row 13's order it answers a recorded stage-12 failure (401 with the failure code; a stale session cookie on a Public route proceeds anonymous and is cleared), no principal (401 `authentication_required`), `Guards.Interactive` (403 `interactive_required`), `Guards.Owner` (403 `owner_required`, or with `core.auth.owner.required=false` an interactive method plus `core:admin`), and `Perm` (403 `permission_denied` naming the missing scope, 403 `interactive_required` for a Sensitive scope refused to a non-interactive method, 401 for the auth-disabled principal). Legacy-atom requirements are resolved through an injected atom resolver before any checker sees them, and anything it cannot resolve is refused. Nothing in production calls it yet; the identity flip wires it into the chain.
