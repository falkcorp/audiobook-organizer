### Added

- `core/internal/outboxhttp`: the outbox's operator routes, written and tested but not mounted (nothing imports the package until F6-03, so no route is served and no behaviour changes). Six routes on the core group: `GET /outbox/dead` lists the dead letters a page at a time (consumer, topic, seq, partition, attempts, the token that says why it died, its idempotency key; never the payload or the key), `POST /outbox/dead/{consumer}/{seq}/retry` hands one back to its consumer alone, `DELETE /outbox/dead/{consumer}/{seq}` discards one, `DELETE /outbox/consumers/{consumer}/backlog` purges a consumer's backlog (interactive sign-in only), and `GET /outbox/consumers` and `GET /events/topics` read the consumers' status and the topic catalog. The dead-letter list and the three writes need `core:system.manage`; the two reads need `core:system.view`.
- Each state change is audited (`core.outbox.dead_retry`, `core.outbox.dead_discard`, `core.outbox.backlog_purge`) before the operation runs, with the principal, the consumer, the topic and the seq, never the message, and nothing is applied that could not be recorded: when that record cannot be written the route answers 503 `unavailable` ("nothing was changed"), does not call the operation, and the failure is counted in `mediacore_audit_write_errors_total{path="sync"}`. An operation that is then refused or fails gets a second record (outcome `failure` for a 404 or 409, `error` otherwise, `partial` for a purge that had already skipped messages), a purge gets a record of what it skipped when it finishes, and a retry or discard that took a letter on another topic than the first record named gets a record naming it. The operation and these records run on contexts a client hang-up cannot cancel, each with a timeout of its own. A route mounted without its operator or its audit logger answers 503 before anything is applied.
- `core/outbox`: `DeadTopic` reads the topic of the dead letter a retry or discard would take from the consumer's keys alone, and `RetryDead` and `DiscardDead` return the topic of the letter they took, so an operator route no longer scans the dead letters of other consumers, and one letter that will not decode no longer fails an operation on another. `PurgeBacklog` reports the messages of the partitions that committed only, also when it fails part-way.
