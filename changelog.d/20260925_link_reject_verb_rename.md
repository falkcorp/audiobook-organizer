### Changed

- The book-duplicates "merge" and dedup-candidate "merge"/"dismiss" endpoints are renamed to say what they actually do: `/audiobooks/duplicates/merge`, `/audiobooks/merge`, and `/dedup/candidates/{...,bulk-,merge-cluster,merge-series}` never moved a file — they always linked the books into a version group — so they are now `.../link`, `.../bulk-link`, `.../link-cluster`, `.../link-series` (matching the naming-audit's "merge vs link verbs" class). The "dismiss" routes (`/audiobooks/duplicates/dismiss`, `/dedup/candidates/{id}/dismiss`, `/dedup/candidates/dismiss-cluster`) are renamed to `.../reject` to match the review-queue's existing "reject" vocabulary. Every old path stays registered and functional as a deprecated alias — no client is broken by this change. `/audiobooks/combine` (a true file-moving merge) and the `/merge/combine-journal` + `/merge/undo/:journal_id` undo trail are unchanged.
