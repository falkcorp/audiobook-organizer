### Fixed

- A restore of a pre-migration checkpoint, and a revert of a restore, no longer re-apply the whole revocation journal. The checkpoint's record now carries the journal head its store had seen (`journal_seq`, read through the new `storage.OpenOptions.JournalHead`, which the kernel binds over `journal.ReadHead`), and the `staged` record of a restore or revert carries the live store's journal head (`Seq`, through the new `Intent.OutgoingSeq` that `backup.StageRestore` and `RevertLastRestore` fill). Both were read by the restore boot but never written, so every boundary was 0 and the journal replayed from its first line, a cost that grew with every revocation ever journaled. A store with no journal head, or a build with no `core/backup` keyspace, still records 0 (replay everything, the safe side); a head that cannot be read refuses the checkpoint or the staging instead of recording 0.
