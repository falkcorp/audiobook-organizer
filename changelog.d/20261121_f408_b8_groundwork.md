### Changed

- The `server-bootstrap` skill and its `bootstrap.sh` exchange the bootstrap token on core's native route, `POST /api/core/v1/auth/bootstrap`, and read the token from `bootstrap-token` (no leading dot) in the state directory. The native answer is the bare body (`key`, `key_id`, `user.username`, `generated_password`) and the request takes `token` only. `bootstrap.sh` also stops grepping a journal field that is no longer logged.
- The unit-lifecycle test signs in through `/api/core/v1/auth/{bootstrap,login}`.

### Added

- The permission-literal ESLint rule of the platform-core UI spec (07 section 7.18): no string literal in a permission position (a `usePermission`, `useAction` or `hasPermission` argument, a `permission` or `*_PERMISSION` property or constant, an API-key scope list). It runs over `web/src` except the five files that still hold a literal until the SPA moves to the generated client.
- Playwright specs for the invite and temp-login fragment links (`/invite#token=` and `/login/temp#token=`), written against the native identity routes.
