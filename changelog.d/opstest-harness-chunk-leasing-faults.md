- New test harness for v3 operations (`pkg/ops/opstest`). `Conformance(t, def, params)` runs an op against an in-memory World (the real Pebble store, synthetic books) at chunk sizes 1, 3 and the op's default with at least 4 workers. It checks nine rules, each as its own subtest: preview writes nothing, history follows its write, no write after the fence is revoked, crash and resume runs every item, a mid-chunk crash re-runs only that chunk, a partition runs on one worker in order, an AppendOnly claim holds, progress never goes backwards, and items are idempotent. Faults (`CancelAt`, `CrashAt`, `CrashMidChunk`, `InsertBelowCursor`, `AbandonAt`, `LoseLeaseAt`) are injected through a new, nil-by-default `RunnerHooks` seam in the batch runner, and a mutation test per rule shows each one fails on a deliberately broken op. Test-only change; no user-visible effect.
