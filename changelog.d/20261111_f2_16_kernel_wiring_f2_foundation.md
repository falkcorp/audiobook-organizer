### Added

- core: the kernel now wires the F2 foundation (F2-16). `module.Manifest` gains `TaskLedger` and `DataMigrations`, and `module.Registrar` gains `Tasks` and `Resources`; P1 checks every manifest's ledger (each entry a kind in the module's own namespace, no kind listed twice) and each data migration's owner, and P7 checks what an enabled module registered (a constructor problem, a kind outside its namespace or its ledger, `PrioritySystem` on a module kind, a `Start` scope outside the module's namespace and core's or a view-class core scope with no reviewed reason, an effect naming a resource kind the module did not declare, a write to another module's resource, and every schedule through `scheduler.ValidateSchedules`). The tasks start rule holds when a module registers a kind or `c/tasks/` stores a run (one bounded read); then P7 upserts the definition snapshots `c/tasks/def/<kind>` and builds the executor, the scheduler and the task server, and Run starts them at steps (5) and (6) and stops them, scheduler first, before the executor drains. In ABO's shape (no kind, no stored run) none of it is built, no `<ns>_ops_*` instrument exists and nothing is written to `c/tasks/`. The audit store is built after P4a and before P4b, its instruments after P5, its writer started before the listener opens and stopped after the executor; the scope catalog is declared at P5, the health registry (`core.storage`, `core.secrets`) and the native task stores are built at the new phase P4c, the data-migration runner's steps and cutover preflight and metrics are wired, and the core sections are registered over a defaults-only config store the executor and scheduler read. Core's two new storage owners, `core/audit` and `core/tasks`, are claimed in every app's store at its first P4b. `hello` registers `hello.count`; `hello.CountTask` and its tasklint allowlist entry are gone. The task routes are built but not mounted: `httpapi` has no raw handler and no auth service yet, so mounting waits for them.
- core: `coretest` task cells: `WithRuns` stores queued runs before the App boots, `Harness.RunTask` starts a run as the harness administrator and waits for its end, `Harness.Runs` lists runs, `Harness.Reboot` boots a new App over the same store, and `AssertModuleInert` checks that a disabled module's runs are listed held and none was dispatched. The harness now starts the executor and scheduler when the start rule holds.
