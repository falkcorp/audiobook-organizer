### Fixed

- Bulk metadata apply: the certainty gate (`internal/applygate`) gains three blocking evidence checks. `cast_in_author` refuses a candidate whose first author is the files' narrator and whose later authors appear nowhere in that credit, the shape of a full-cast drama credited to its writer plus the cast (13 Big Finish dramas on the 2026-09-13 preview). A narrator who is a later author (an author reading their own co-written book) is not blocked, names are compared in full when both sides give a first name, and the check steps aside when the provider names its own narrator. `series_number_lost` refuses a title overwrite that drops the stored title's volume number ("Empire of Man 04 - We Few" -> "We Few"); its exception for a number kept as the series position is behind `KeepNumberViaSeries`, off. `partial_book` refuses one part or disc of a book matched to the whole book, a box set matched to one volume, and a batch where a sibling folder holds another part of the same candidate. A part marker that opens a range ("Disc 1-3") counts as the whole book, and a single-file book's file name is read for a marker. All three batch paths (preview, cached batch op, batch-apply-candidates) build the sibling index before the first apply from the whole candidate source (every book with cached candidates, or every row of the operation), never from the request's book list, so a preview and an apply of any subset, including a resumed run, see the same siblings; a failed cache listing fails the op instead of shrinking the index.
