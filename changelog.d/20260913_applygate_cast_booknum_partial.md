### Fixed

- Bulk metadata apply: the certainty gate (`internal/applygate`) gains three blocking evidence checks. `cast_in_author` refuses a candidate whose first author is the files' narrator and whose later authors appear nowhere in that credit, the shape of a full-cast drama credited to its writer plus the cast (13 Big Finish dramas on the 2026-09-13 preview). A narrator who is a later author (an author reading their own co-written book) is not blocked, names are compared in full when both sides give a first name, and the check steps aside when the provider names its own narrator. `series_number_lost` refuses a title overwrite that drops the stored title's volume number ("Empire of Man 04 - We Few" -> "We Few"); its exception for a number kept as the series position is behind `KeepNumberViaSeries`, off. `partial_book` refuses one part or disc of a book matched to the whole book, a box set matched to one volume, and a batch where a sibling folder holds another part of the same candidate. All three batch paths (preview, cached batch op, batch-apply-candidates) build the same sibling claim index before the first apply.
