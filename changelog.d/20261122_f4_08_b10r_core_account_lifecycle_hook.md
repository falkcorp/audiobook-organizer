### Added

- Core identity now has an account lifecycle hook, the first half of keeping the Cloudflare Access guest list in step with guests once identity is on. A module that declares `AuthLifecycle` in its manifest registers one set of callbacks, once, from `Register`: invite accepted, status changed and e-mail changed. Each is called after the write has committed, with ids and the few facts the module needs (the invite's creator and whether that creator held admin at the accept, the old and new status, the old and new address) and never a password, token or key. A callback cannot veto the write, and one that panics is logged and fails nothing. Nothing is registered yet: the books side that adds and removes Cloudflare entries follows.
- Accepting an invite now has the helper that takes an address back from accounts that only typed it in, in the accept's own commit, with an audit detail and a warning, ready for the change that makes an emailed invite bind its address. It is not called yet: an accepted invite still stores its address unverified and touches no other account.
