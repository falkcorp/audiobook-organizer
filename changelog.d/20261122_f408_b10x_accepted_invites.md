### Fixed

- The Users page's "Accepted Invites (last 30 days)" table works again once accounts live in core (identity flip, F4-08 B10x). `GET /api/v1/users/invites` answered an empty `accepted` list under the flip, because an accepted invite is core's and books kept no record of it, so the owner could not see whether a guest (a Lissen phone user) got in. The list is now read from books' own accept rows (`acceptedinvite:`, see the B10x2 entry), joined to the Cloudflare guest-policy outcome rows (`guestpolicyout:`) by invite id, over HEAD's 30 days and newest first, in HEAD's JSON member names. The gate is unchanged and runs first: a caller that may not list invites (a non-admin session, or an API key without `users.manage`) is refused as at HEAD and sees no guest; with authentication off the list is served as at HEAD; the read never writes.
