### Fixed

- The Users page's "Accepted Invites (last 30 days)" table works again once accounts live in core (identity flip, F4-08 B10x). `GET /api/v1/users/invites` answered an empty `accepted` list under the flip, because an accepted invite is core's and books kept no record of it, so the owner could not see whether a guest (a Lissen phone user) got in. The list is now read from books' own guest-policy outcome rows (`guestpolicyout:`), over HEAD's 30 days and newest first, in HEAD's JSON member names: `username` (the accepted account's, read from core), `accepted_user_id`, `guest_policy_status`, `guest_policy_detail`, `guest_policy_at`, and `accepted_at`. The gate is unchanged and runs first: a caller that may not list invites (a non-admin session, or an API key without `users.manage`) is refused as at HEAD and sees no guest; with authentication off the list is served as at HEAD; the read never writes. Known differences from HEAD, all of them omissions: `email`, `email_binding`, `sso_grant` and `displaced_user_ids` are not recorded under core identity and are left out; `accepted_at` is the time the guest-policy sync last reported (seconds after the accept); an accept whose invite named no address and an accept made before the flip leave no row to list. New `GuestPolicyStore.ListGuestPolicyOutcomesSince`.
