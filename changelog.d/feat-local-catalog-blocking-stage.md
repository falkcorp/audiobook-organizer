- Candidate search can now look a book up in the harvested author catalog before it asks any provider, with no provider call. If the book has an ASIN the catalog is read by it; otherwise each of the book's author credits, in order, is taken to that author's block by its exact folded name (or by a spelling-variant alias the harvest files: a dropped middle name, initials, "Last, First") and the block's titles are matched against every title the book is known by, with digits required to agree and a capped edit distance. Entries no harvest lists any more are skipped; a block over 2,000 entries is skipped and counted; a name shared by two Audible authors is skipped and counted unless the authority lists or the harvest already settled which one the book's author is. What it finds joins the candidates as review-only candidates ("Audible Catalog (local)", tagged B1 or B2 with the position of the credit that found it): bulk apply never applies one, only an owner review does, and the provider calls a search makes are unchanged. The setting is `metadata_fetch.catalog_stage` and ships OFF; turn it on with `PUT /api/v1/config` `{"metadata_fetch":{"catalog_stage":true}}` after reading the `catalog.coverage-report` number. The candidate-fetch op's progress and final lines now end with `catalog_candidates`, `provider_candidates`, `oversize_block` and `homonym_skipped`, counting that attempt's searches. The harvest files the alias rows for the entries it stores, and the end of every harvest run files them for older entries and for spellings the authority lists know. A book whose title is a person's name, or its own author's, is now searched by the folder holding its files when that folder names a real title. A book filled from a catalog candidate carries Audible's source tag and rank, and its year is read as the audiobook release year.
