### Changed

- Core AI: the ai kind's endpoint validator is now installed by every `ai.NewRouter` (`RouterOptions.ValidateEndpoints` is removed), so a Put, Test or import of an ai instance is refused for a wildcard or unknown capability tick, a concurrency below 1, a bad locality or a bad feature, with nothing for the caller to remember. A second Router over one Registry is refused. Dark: ABO builds no Router before the F7-09 flip.
- Core AI: `batch_api` may now be required of embedding work as well as text work, so books' `books.embed_text_batch` registers and the catalog holds all 13 books capabilities. Batches of embeddings are supported end to end: `ai.EmbedItem`, `Router.SubmitBatch` for an embed capability (pinned model required, `ErrEmbedModelNotPinned`), `/v1/embeddings` lines in the openai adapter and `BatchResult.Embed` on the way back. `SubmitBatch` refuses a capability that is neither text nor embed work, and an item of the wrong kind, with `ai.ErrBatchKind`, before any job row exists.
