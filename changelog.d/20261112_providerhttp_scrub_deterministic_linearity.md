### Fixed

- core/tests: `TestScrub_HostileBodyIsBounded` no longer times the scrubber. A wall-clock growth ratio flaked three times on CI under `-race` and coverage even at a 128x bound, because cache and GC effects at 1 MiB move a linear scanner by more than any bound that is still far below quadratic. The scrubber's forward scans now report the bytes they walk to an optional, test-only `workMeter`, and the test asserts at most 4 bytes examined per input byte on 14 hostile shapes, plus a bound on the excerpt path for a 16 MiB body. Measured growth from 32 KiB to 1 MiB, with and without `-race`, is linear (about 2x per doubling), so `scrubber.text` had no bug. Two mutants that rescan (an unbounded URL authority scan, and resuming a name at the next byte) fail the new test within a second.
