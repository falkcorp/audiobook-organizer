### Fixed

- Every repo-owned typed `packages.Load` now type-checks on one goroutine, through the new `core/archtest/typedload` (a copy in `tools/fixerstores/typedload`, a module of its own, kept identical by `internal/arch.TestTypedloadCopyMatches`). The tasklint fix (#4909) covered one loader; the other ones share its defect: `packages.Load` type-checks independent packages concurrently and they share lazily expanded generic instances (go/types leaves an `iter.Seq[T]` in a dependency's signature unexpanded, from source as from export data), which go1.27's `isComplete` reads without the lock `unpack` writes under (golang/go#81122, golang/go#82614). Switched: the archtest guards (`ResumeContinueCheckpoints`, `RunItemsDeclareParallelism`), tasklint, `TestRunItemsCallsDeclareConcurrency`, `TestResultTypesWithUserIDsAreGated`, the jobs capability scan, the jobs write oracle, `tools/fixerstores`, and the two run-once `book_file_duration` codemods. `typedload.Load` takes the caller's `packages.Config` (NeedDeps, Overlay, Tests, Env and BuildFlags honoured) and returns the same result; with NeedDeps every package of the graph is checked from source in dependency order, in one type universe. Delete `typedload` for plain `packages.Load` once a released toolchain carries the go/types fix.
