### Fixed

- `TestFixerStores_NoFixerReachesAStoreExceptThroughTheShell` was red on main with 15 reports (one `OperationQueueStore` sink and fourteen deps) after the F5-18c books deps builders landed, because they were phantom: `booksGate.do` and `gateQuiet` take the store call of every gated writer method as a closure and only call it, and VTA, which has no call-site context for a func value a shared helper invokes, handed the fixer path through `booksJobsStore.CreateOperationChange` every closure of the group 2, 3 and 6 writers. `tools/fixerstores` now treats the two trampolines as join points (the way it already treats `registry.RunItems`): their call-graph edges are not followed and the closure named at each call site is, so a real store or dep call inside a gated closure on a fixer's path is still reported. The allowlist is unchanged, 1 sink and 14 deps as before F5-18c; 5464 reachable functions drop to 5040.
