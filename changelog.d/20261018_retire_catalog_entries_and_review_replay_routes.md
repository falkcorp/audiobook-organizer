Retired the author-catalog read routes (GET `/api/v1/catalog/entries` and `/api/v1/catalog/entries/:id`) and POST `/api/v1/review/replay-approved`. Nothing in the web app, scripts or agent tooling called them. They now answer 410 Gone for one release, with their usual authentication and permission checks still running first, and the handlers and their tests are deleted. The catalog stubs have no replacement. The owner-kept diagnostics (`GET /api/v1/op-defs/:id`, `/ai/capabilities`, `/ai/endpoints/status`, `/tools/:name/status`) are untouched.

The replay stub points callers at `POST /api/v1/review/bulk` with `{"action":"approve","ids":[...]}`, listing the approved items with `GET /api/v1/review/items?status=approved`. Approving an already-approved item again runs its recorded action once `review_apply_enabled` is on, so a decision made while apply was off still executes. The approve note shown while apply is off now says to approve the item again after enabling apply, since replay no longer exists. `CatalogStore.ListEntries`, `CatalogListQuery` and `EditionGroupMembers` were deleted, because the retired route was their only production caller and nothing else reads them. No store interface methods were deleted and no data is touched.

`POST /api/v1/review/bulk` approve no longer stops at the first item whose apply errors with a bare 500 (which left earlier items applied but unreported). It carries on and returns 200 with a `failed` list of `{id, reason}`; failed items keep their status, so resending their id retries them. If an apply succeeds but the `applied` status write then fails (retried once), the item is reported in a separate `applied_unrecorded` list and must not be resent. The response sets `apply_disabled: true` when `review_apply_enabled` is off and nothing was applied. The regroup lane's toast names failed items, unrecorded items and an apply-off approve, and the failures stay in the kind's report panel as clickable entries until dismissed.

An apply now claims its item first (a new `applying` status, taken atomically through the `ReviewItemApplyClaimer` capability), so two concurrent approves of the same item apply it once; a claim that crashes mid-apply is taken over after 15 minutes. Approving or rejecting an item that is already applied now answers 409 `REVIEW_ALREADY_APPLIED` (single routes) or lists it under `skipped` (bulk), instead of re-running the handler or moving an applied item to rejected.
