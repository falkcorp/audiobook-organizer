### Fixed

- Audiobookshelf single sign-on through Cloudflare Access keeps working when the server runs under core identity. Under core identity every verified Access person on the Audiobookshelf routes was refused with a 403 (`cf-resolve-unavailable-under-core-identity`), which broke the browser sign-in and the app's OpenID hand-off at the flip. A verified person is now resolved by core's account resolver under the same admission policy the Access sign-in uses: a listed person finds, links or gets a core account, a person who is not listed or whose address is unverified gets the usual 403 and nothing is created, an inactive or restricted account gets the account-not-active 403, and a store fault is a 500 rather than a 401 or 403. The Audiobookshelf routes now verify the Access token with the same verifier the Access sign-in holds (same Cloudflare keys, issuer and audience), and nothing in books' own user store is read or written on this path. Servers not under core identity behave as before.
