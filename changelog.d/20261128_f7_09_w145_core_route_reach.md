Fixed core's integrations and library routes being wider than HEAD's reach before the F7-09 mount, kind by kind. The six instance routes (list, get, create, update, delete, release hold) and `browseLibrary` now require `rbac.OperatorRequirement()`, the seven core scopes of HEAD's settings.manage atom, so a principal holding only HEAD's integrations.manage atom, or only `core:integrations.manage` and `core:settings.manage`, is refused them. Health and the two test routes mount `core:integrations.manage` as a floor and apply a per-kind rule in the handler, which the route documentation and the handler's backstop both read: ai and notify (health and test) and metadata's test ask the seven scopes (so the integrations atom no longer reaches notify or ai health), download answers 404 for every principal that passes the floor (served by the legacy `/api/v1/deluge/status` and `/api/v1/deluge/test-connection` until the W6 per-kind co-requirement lands), metadata health answers 404 naming the legacy `GET /api/v1/metadata/providers/throttles` (its payload carries the provider hold, which HEAD refuses a settings-atom key), and indexer answers 404. Create and update stay open to a non-interactive settings-atom key, as HEAD's `PUT /api/v1/config` was. The legacy `GET /api/v1/metadata/providers/throttles`, `POST /api/v1/ai/test-connection` and `/api/v1/deluge/*` routes stay alive, each pinned by a boot cell and a guard that fails if one is retired. None of the core routes is mounted yet, so nothing is served differently until F7-09 mounts them.
