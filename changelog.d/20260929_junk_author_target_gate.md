- Junk authors: the author a book is moved to must now pass the same "is this a person?" test as the author it replaces. A trial plan was moving about 5% of books onto a name that was junk itself: a genre ("Lesbian Romance"), a slug ("the-final-strife"), a "The ..." title or series ("The Complete", "The Rosharan System"), a track or volume label ("Avatars Dance 1", "B01 Her"), a franchise ("Star Wars", "Stargate SG-1") or a site tag ("abooks"). These new checks can also match real credits ("The Dalai Lama", "The Beatles", "50 Cent"), so a book they flag is only ever moved to a real author found in its tags, folder or provider; with no such evidence the credit is kept and the row is held, never removed. A credit like "Dante King (Dragon Born)", where the parenthesis is a series in the library, is now recognized and moved to "Dante King" (and linked to the "Dragon Born" series); a narrator in parentheses ("Kevin Hearne (Luke Daniels)") is left alone. A title ending "by <author>" now names that author when the author already has books ("dramatised by", "music by", "voiced by" and similar credits are not taken). No new author is ever created from a name that opens with "The", "A" or "An". Doctor Who books are now recognized in more forms and stay hands-off: the Doctor ranges ("The Thirteenth Doctor Adventures", "The War Doctor", "13th Doctor"), "Dr Who" / "Dr. Who", and the Big Finish spin-offs (Gallifrey, the Daleks, Jago & Litefoot, The Diary of River Song, Bernice Summerfield, Counter-Measures, The Paternoster Gang, Missy, UNIT, Blake's 7).
