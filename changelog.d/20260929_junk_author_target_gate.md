- Junk authors: the author a book is moved to must now pass the same "is this a person?" test as the author it replaces. A trial plan was moving about 5% of books onto a name that was junk itself: a genre ("Lesbian Romance"), a slug ("the-final-strife"), a "The ..." title or series ("The Complete", "The Rosharan System"), a track or volume label ("Avatars Dance 1", "B01 Her"), a franchise ("Star Wars", "Stargate SG-1") or a site tag ("abooks"). A credit like "Dante King (Dragon Born)", where the parenthesis is a series in the library, is now recognized and moved to "Dante King"; a narrator in parentheses ("Kevin Hearne (Luke Daniels)") is left alone. A title ending "by <author>" now names that author when the author already has books. No new author is ever created from a name that opens with "The", "A" or "An". Books from the Doctor ranges ("The Thirteenth Doctor Adventures", "The War Doctor", "13th Doctor") are now hands-off like every other Doctor Who / Big Finish / Torchwood book.
