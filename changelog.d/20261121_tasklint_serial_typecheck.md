### Fixed

- `TestTasklint_Books` and `TestTasklint_F518ListsOnlyFlaggedFiles` no longer fail intermittently under `-race` with `WARNING: DATA RACE` between `(*types.Named).unpack` and `(*types.Checker).isComplete`. go1.27's type checker reads a lazily expanded imported type without the lock its expansion writes under, and `packages.Load` type-checks independent packages of one call concurrently over those shared types (golang/go#81122, golang/go#82614; the fix, CL 841585, is not in a released Go). `tasklint.Analyze` now loads the import graph and export files through `go/packages` but parses in parallel and type-checks the packages on one goroutine, in dependency order, in one type universe, so no two checkers meet a shared type. The findings are unchanged (32 packages, 9 findings). Delete `core/archtest/tasklint/load.go` for a plain `packages.Load` once a released toolchain carries the fix.
