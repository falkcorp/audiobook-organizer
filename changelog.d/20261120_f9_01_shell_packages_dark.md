### Added

#### Platform-core F9-01: `@mediacore/ui-kit`, `@mediacore/shell` and the hello SPA (dark)

`core/web` gains two workspace packages. `@mediacore/ui-kit` holds the theme factory (one `createTheme` call over merged options, with a WCAG AA contrast test across both colour schemes), the theme boot script, the keyboard-chord hook and the four-state `DataState`. `@mediacore/shell` holds `createShell`, `defineModule` with manifest-keyed types, nav and go-key tables, the version intersection with the stale-build banner, realtime, toasts, the task tracker, auth, `SchemaForm`, the `/testing` entry (`renderInShell`, `FakeRealtime`) and the `/vite` presets (`mediacore`, `mediacoreLibrary`). On the Go side, `ui.Bundle`, `ui.Handler` (index CSP with the boot-script hash, immutable assets, `X-App-Build`), the UI metrics scope and the manifest TypeScript generator land, and the kernel mounts a non-zero `Config.UI` as the "/" owner. The hello example ships a shell SPA embedded under `-tags embed_frontend`, with a Playwright bundle check and the core half of the stale-build spec. No ABO page imports the shell yet: the pages move in F9-04 after the F4 to F7 flips.
