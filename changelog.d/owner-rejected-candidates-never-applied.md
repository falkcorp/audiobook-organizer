### Fixed

- A metadata candidate the owner rejected (`POST /metadata/batch-reject-candidates`) is never applied again, by any path. Before, the rejection was only a key the batch fetch's own pick read, so the rejected candidate stayed first in the book's cached row and six paths applied it: a pinless `metadata.batch-apply-cached`, every review-page apply button (row, bulk and the hashless bulk marker), `batch-apply-candidates` and `metadata.apply-when-scanned` against another op's still-"matched" result, `maintenance.auto-match-transcribed`, the nightly metadata upgrade (which overwrote filled fields), and `POST /metadata/bulk-fetch`.
- The certainty gate has a hard `owner_rejected` leg (and `owner_rejection_check_failed` when the rejection list cannot be read) that no owner-review pin lifts; un-reject the candidate to apply it. The transcription auto-match and the bulk fetch check rejections themselves.
- Rejecting or un-rejecting a candidate re-orders the book's cached row (rejected candidates last, by the merge ranking), and a search that replaces the row (including the search dialog's fetch) ranks it the same way, so a refetch cannot put a rejected candidate back first. A one-time background pass re-orders every existing row that holds a rejected candidate (`system:migration:owner_rejected_rerank_v1_done`); it never removes a candidate.
- Rejection keys are built and matched in one place, case-insensitively like the cached row's own de-duplication; existing keys keep working, and un-reject removes a rejection whatever case it was stored in.
- The review rail marks a row whose candidate the owner rejected (`owner_rejected` on the review list).
- The single-book apply (`POST /audiobooks/:id/apply-metadata`, used by the search dialogs, and its queued background form) refuses a rejected candidate with 409 `owner_rejected`. The search dialogs mark a rejected candidate "Rejected by you" (`apply_check.owner_rejected`) and do not stage it.
