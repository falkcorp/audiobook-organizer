- The nightly "clean old backup files" job and the retired manual "Cleanup Backups" job no longer exist as two separate implementations of the same walk. The walk that skips the application's own backup and OpenLibrary dump directories, ages a backup by the time in its name and removes it now lives in one place, `sweep.RemoveOldBackupFiles`, and the nightly job calls it. Which files the nightly job deletes does not change: only the names this app writes (`.bak-<unix>[-N]` and the older `.bak-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS`), and only past the retention window. A file that merely has `.bak-` in its name is kept at any age.
