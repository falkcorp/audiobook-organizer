- Repairs, junk authors (`maintenance.repair-junk-authors`), round 2 of bad relink targets. Each is refused at plan time and again in Apply's backstop, and the row is held with the refusal as its reason:
  - A work title stored as an author row. When a book title or series name in the library equals the target's name, the fixer checks who those books are credited to. If they belong to another real author, the target is a work and is refused: "Cthulhu Armageddon" (C. T. Phipps), "Wraith Knight", "Jane Yellowrock", "Pet Sematary", "Night Angel", "Small Gods", and others. Before this, a junk row passed simply because it had other books.
    - Real authors whose names are also series names ("Brent Weeks", "Douglas Adams", "Glynn Stewart") still pass. Some credits are not treated as another author's evidence: a strong-junk row, a variant spelling of the name, a reader filed as the author ("Cressida Cowell" series under David Tennant), or a swapped folder parse ("Mistborn" / "Brandon Sanderson").
  - "Person (Work)" composites: "Cathfach (A (Not So) Simple Fetch Quest)" now resolves to the existing "Cathfach", and the composite row is never a target.
  - New authors are never created from a name with brackets or an author-title hyphen join ("Raymond L. Weil-Star Cross[1-5]").
- `authorjunk`: parser and form placeholders made only of placeholder words ("parse author", "author name", "Test Author", "NO IDEA") are now junk.
