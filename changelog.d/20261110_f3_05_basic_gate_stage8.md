### Added

- core: stage 8, the HTTP Basic perimeter gate, in `core/httpapi/basicgate.go` (dark: built and tested, not in any chain until the config flip F3-07, and gin's `basicauth.go` stays). It reads the effective `core.http.basic_gate.enabled` and `.username` and resolves `.password` through a one-method seam (`BasicGatePassword`) that F3-07 binds to the secret store. Its exempt set is HEAD's, per route and never per mount: the `/livez` and `/readyz` probes, `OwnAuth` legacy routes for a request gin routes to one of their entries (so no `..` spelling, HEAD method or generalised-pattern miss widens it), `BasicExempt` legacy routes, and the "/" owner's static-asset predicate on its own answers and unmatched paths. A native route never takes the asset rule. An enabled gate with no usable password (unset, cleared, empty or unreadable) refuses every non-exempt request with HEAD's 401 challenge, reports health `basic_gate_no_password` and logs an ERROR naming `core.http.basic_gate.password`, never a value. `TestBasicGate_EmptyPasswordRefuses` is the property test of 05-http-api §12.
