- New operation `metafetch.asin-backfill`: walks every book with no ASIN and looks it up on Audible only (never Google Books or Open Library), at 4 concurrent books through the shared Audible rate limiter. An ASIN is written only when exactly one Audible product passes a strict gate: equal title (or a one-sided subtitle difference, never a volume number), a matching author, no series-position or runtime conflict, and at least one corroboration (audiobook ISBN, runtime within 10%, or series name and position). Ambiguous books and books with no confident match are left empty, and a no-match is remembered for 30 days (configurable) so repeat runs skip it. Writes only empty, unlocked ASIN fields, records per-book history, and defaults to a dry run.
