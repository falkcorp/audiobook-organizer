### Added

- Under core identity (`server.WithIdentity`, which nothing sets yet) the owner gate, the Repairs owner status and owner apply, an open event stream's "does the credential still stand" check, and the user-merge undo and resume routes now run against core's principal instead of books' own records. All of it is dark: a server without the option behaves exactly as before. The owner proof is the Cloudflare Access extension's principal carrying the owner assurance (`cfaccess.OwnerProofWhyNotCore`), with HEAD's refusal wording and permission leg; it deliberately does not use core's owner fallback, which with `core.auth.owner.required=false` would let any interactive `core:admin` principal through where HEAD refuses everyone but the Access owner while `owner_email` is set. An open stream re-resolves its principal through `Identity.Refresh` and fails closed with none. Once the account store is core's, undoing or resuming a merge answers 409 `merge_permanent` before any journal is read (`usermerge.Service.CheckAction`, fed by `usermerge.WithIdentityFlipped`). Tests pin the same-email case (two admins sharing the owner's address, one linked to Access): merging the linked account away needs the acknowledgement, the other direction does not, and nothing merges them without an apply.
