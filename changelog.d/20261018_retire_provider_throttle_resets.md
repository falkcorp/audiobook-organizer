Two provider-throttle reset routes are retired: `DELETE /api/v1/metadata/providers/throttles` (clear every hold) and `DELETE /api/v1/metadata/providers/throttles/:id` (clear one hold). Nothing in the web app, scripts or the AudioBooth client called them. They now answer 410 Gone for one release and name no replacement, because nothing replaces a manual reset: a hold now ends only when its timer runs out or, for the kinds of hold a success may release, a later successful call to that provider ends it. `GET /api/v1/metadata/providers/throttles` (the read-only view of what is held) and `GET /api/v1/metadata/fields` are kept by owner decision. The two handlers, the registry's `Clear` and `ClearAll` methods and their tests are deleted. No store methods were deleted: the throttle store methods are still used when a successful call releases a hold. The stubs keep their edit-metadata permission guard, so signed-out callers still get 401 and read-only users 403.
