### Fixed

- The last-admin rule of core's auth service now guards the role definition that makes the other administrator an administrator, when it scans for another holder. A role edit that took the admin permission from that role could commit between auth's read and auth's commit, and a user demotion, deactivation or delete committing right after it left no active administrator. The role store gains an optional guard (`rbac.RoleGuarder`, implemented by the native store) and `rbac.Service.GuardRole`; a role store without one makes the write fail closed. The two exits of the rule that decide before that scan, and `rbac.Service.DeleteRole`, are not covered yet. Nothing wires the role-holder source yet, so none of this is reachable today.
