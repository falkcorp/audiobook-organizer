### Fixed

- The last-admin rule of core's auth service now also guards the role definition that makes the other administrator an administrator. Before, a role edit that took the admin permission away from that role could commit between auth's read and auth's commit, and a user demotion, deactivation or delete committing right after it left no active administrator. The edit now fails the user write's commit and the retry refuses it with the last-admin error, and the other order was already caught by the role edit's own check. The role store gains an optional guard (`rbac.RoleGuarder`, implemented by the native store) and `rbac.Service.GuardRole`; a role store without one makes the write fail closed. Nothing wires the role-holder source yet, so this closes the gap before it could be reached.
