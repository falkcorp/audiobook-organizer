### Fixed

#### The apply gate's title check needs the whole title, not one shared segment

`checkTitle` in `internal/applygate/evidence.go` scored title agreement 1.0 when the candidate's main title equalled ANY segment of the stored title (or of the file or folder name). Two volumes of one storyline share the segment before the colon, so with the runtime inside 5% and the author agreeing (the two agreements the gate needs) a bulk apply wrote the wrong volume onto the book unattended: "Hush: Part 1" took the metadata of "Hush: Part 2" and the reverse, the same with roman numerals ("Part I" and "Part II", where `tokens` also dropped the one-letter numeral), and "Batman: Dark Victory" took the metadata of "Batman: The Long Halloween" (whose subtitle only mentions the other storyline) and the reverse. The comparison now lives in `internal/applygate/titlecompare.go` and is symmetric in its two texts: numbers are kept as values (digits, number words, roman numerals, kept apart by kind: part, disc and track against book, volume and a number that is part of the title), a shared segment with a different remainder on each side is a conflict, and a conflict is a `title_disagrees` block. Titles that differ only by a one-sided number, a one-sided prefix, an edition suffix, a trailing subtitle, a declared series name, an author or narrator name, a list ordinal ("08 - Title") or a multi-part suffix ("Title - 001") still agree or stay neutral as before.

#### Batch apply from candidate-fetch results no longer re-applies an applied book

`planOpResultApply` (the planner for `/metadata/batch-apply-candidates` and its preview) lacked the already-applied check `planCachedApply` has. The op result's own "applied" status is written only when this path applies the book, so a book applied since the fetch by the cached bulk apply, the review lane or the single-book dialog still had a "matched" result and was applied again: file work, a tag write, an iTunes write-back and a history entry. It now comes back `already_applied` (skipped, not an error), and the preview reports it as skipped.
