Retired the two merge-journal undo routes, `POST /api/v1/merge/undo/:journal_id` and `POST /api/v1/merge/sibling-undo/:journal_id`. Nothing in the app called them. They now answer 410 Gone for one release, and their handlers (`UndoCombine` and `UndoSiblingMove` on the duplicates handler), the handler test and the matching methods on the handler's merge-service interface are deleted. No store methods were deleted: the handlers only reached the merge service. The two read-only journal lists (`GET /api/v1/merge/combine-journal` and `GET /api/v1/merge/sibling-journal`) are kept.

The combine-undo stub points callers at `POST /api/v1/operations/:id/revert`, which reverses a combine a Repairs fixer made through its recorded pointer row. A manual combine (`POST /api/v1/audiobooks/combine`) and the other combines have no such row, and a sibling-move undo has no replacement, so those can no longer be reversed over HTTP; a follow-up task records the gap. The duplicates `merge` and `dismiss` verb aliases were already retired behind 410 by the earlier duplicates cleanup.
