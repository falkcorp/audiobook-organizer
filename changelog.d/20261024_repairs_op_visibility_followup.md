Security: the Audible read-status fixer (`maintenance.audible-read-status`) now needs `users.manage`, the same as the master-list import (owner decision 2026-10-24: admins only). Its plan and apply writes any user's listening state, so an editor can no longer plan, apply, owner-apply or read its plan rows, and a Repairs operation of that fixer is readable only to a caller who holds `users.manage`. A Repairs operation whose fixer this build no longer registers now also asks for `users.manage`, because the removed fixer could have been one of the two read-status imports.

Security: the operations v3 routes and the activity log no longer show a Repairs run to a caller who may not use its fixer. On `/api/v3/ops`, `repairs.plan` and `repairs.apply` runs the caller may not read are left out of `runs` (the page and `total` are counted without them, short pages are refilled), `counts` (every grouping, so no count admits a hidden run), `timeline`, `defs` (`last_run` is null) and the `events` stream (each subscriber is filtered on its own permissions, including the replay after a reconnect); `runs/:id` and `runs/:id/rows` answer 403, and so do cancel and approve for a caller other than the run's starter. In the activity log, `GET /activity` leaves out every row of such an operation (the reporter's rows, the engine's own log lines and digests that name it) and counts its total without them; `GET /operations/:id/activity` answers 403, and `POST /operations/activity/merged` drops such members and answers 403 when none is readable. Other operations and their activity are unchanged.
