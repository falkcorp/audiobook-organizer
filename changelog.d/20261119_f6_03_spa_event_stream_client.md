### Added

- The browser's new realtime client for `GET /api/core/v1/events`, `web/src/services/coreEvents/`, written ahead of the events flip and not imported by anything yet (the old `eventSourceManager` still runs the app). It reads the stream with `fetch` and a `ReadableStream`, sends `Last-Event-ID` on every reconnect, backs off 3 s doubling to 60 s with jitter and never sooner than `Retry-After` (and never gives up, where the old manager stopped after five tries), raises `ApiAuthRedirectError` on an `opaqueredirect` bounce instead of following it, reconnects after `core.stream.closed.v1`, tells subscribers to refetch on `core.stream.reset.v1`, shows refusals with their status and code, drops a stream that goes silent, and opens a stream only while the tab is visible. It exposes `useTopic`, `useResync`, `useStreamClosed` and `useStreamStatus`, with a dedicated keyed stream for a run's log lines.
